Counting: 14 × 14 × 14 × 14 = 38,416 boxes

“sat”, “mat” and “cat” all use the same row t in Table 1 and row a in Table 2. That's sharing.

The catch: a shared box can't be counted. Row t in Table 1 works for many words at once, so there's no single “times seen” to write in it. Your model needs another way to find these numbers.
